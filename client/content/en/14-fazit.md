A robust fitness strategy remains unspectacular:

- enough protein,
- mostly good food,
- controlled calorie balance for the diet phase and mass phase,
- progressive strength training,
- sufficient endurance and everyday exercise,
- sleep and regeneration,
- keep major avoidable risks low,
- realistic routines,
- good sources instead of hype.

The Fitness Compass is useful when it simplifies decisions and supports repeatable behavior.
