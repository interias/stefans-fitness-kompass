YouTube is intended here as a practical and classification source, not as a primary source. The following overview is not a seal of quality or a permanent ranking. It describes what a channel can be useful for in practice, which limits should be taken into account and why statements from it are always checked against guidelines, reviews, position papers and your own context. Status of the editorial classification: 2026-05-30.

## 13.1 Evidence-based and strong in training

| source | role | Often useful for | Border |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <a href="https://rpstrength.com/pages/team/michael-israetel">Renaissance Periodization / Dr. Mike Israetel</a> and <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@RenaissancePeriodization">RP YouTube</a> | Coach and practice source with strong hypertrophy systematics | Training volume, periodization, RIR, diet and muscle building logic | Tone, humor, depth of detail and model logic are not suitable for every target group; Check concrete figures against your own recovery and specialist sources |
| <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@JeffNippard">Jeff Nippard</a> | visually strong bridge between studies and gym practice | Exercise execution, hypertrophy, study classification, training planning | good secondary source but not primary source; individual videos can be context-dependent due to topicality or sponsorships |
| <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@HouseofHypertrophy">House of Hypertrophy</a> | research-backed deep dives | Sober classification of training studies, hypertrophy and strength issues | Proximity to studies does not replace target group testing; individual study designs often remain small or indirect |
| <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@Sean_Nalewanyj">Sean Nalewanyj</a> | Practice and myth filter | Muscle building, nutrition, supplements, social media claims | Helpful against hype, but always think about the product and coaching context |

## 13.3 Entertainment and Inspiration

Use these channels more for motivation, entertainment or ideas. They are not expert sources for health, diet, exercise or supplements. The more emphasis is placed on challenge logic, body presentation, product sales or extreme routines, the more important it is to distance yourself from your own planning:
